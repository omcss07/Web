Ocean Snack

There's a bitter feeling of not being able to "do" while believing that you "are doing," of not giving more chances to life, or simply believing that you value yourself while knowing it's not true. Why doubt so much? Is it so hard to be happy? Why do we plant such corrupt ideas in our heads and make others compare us, or worse, compare ourselves to each other? When you see someone who has given so much for you, even though they have their problems, you want to give back everything they've given. It's irritating but at the same time intriguing trying to understand the mind of someone who helps but won't let themselves be helped - what's the point of helping others if you end up sacrificing all your health? What's the point of saying things with the aim of helping if you don't even apply them yourself? It's worrying, it's strange, it's simply illogical. What goes through their heads? Helping these people, does it really help them or not? Maybe they simply have their own perspective of selfishness and pride that doesn't allow them to listen to others; not listening to others isn't wrong, but the moment you don't even listen to yourself everything loses meaning, if I'm not going to turn to myself or others. It's concerning how these people feel, giving everything for others but then saying "There's a limit where we must know when to help and when not to," "You're going to corrupt yourself if you continue like this"... I don't know how it's possible to leave all these things behind and be a new, happy person. Sometimes, even though questioning things moves humanity forward, it can bring it to its lowest point. What would happen if we were all happy? "Facts do not cease to exist because they are ignored" (Aldous Huxley). What is one doing wrong? For these people to truly realize their worth, things exist for a purpose, and ignoring them leads to nothing but delaying necessary progress to live happily. It's painful to walk on a path of hot coal, but if at the end of it there's a feeling of freedom, gratitude, and happiness, we must take the risk. Where does this strange thought of possibly failing come from? How do they not realize their true worth and the capabilities they have, capabilities that make others happy, capabilities that become disabled by something as stupid as overthinking? Anyway. Thank you to all those people who take the time to care for others, thank you for what you do for everyone, but it's annoying and absurd how unable they are to love themselves, or the courage they have to say they love themselves while being broken inside. I love you all, without you society would be nothing. Thank you for those sacrifices that everyone makes even though as human beings we still can't understand that behavior, thank you for defying the survival instinct and bringing something new that is implicitly love, thank you to all those beings who move the world, you are everything.
